# Transport privacy policy
Updated 24 October 2026. This policy covers the Transport app for iPhone and Android and the web app at nsw-transport-bot.sites.tab.bot.

Transport has no accounts. It does not ask for your name, email or phone number, and it has no ads, no analytics and no third party tracking. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

## What the server keeps

* A random ID for your install. The app makes it on your phone the first time it opens. It is not your device's advertising ID and is not linked to who you are. The server uses it to keep your saved trips, commutes, watched trips and alerts together.

* Saved trips and commutes you create, with their names and stops, until you delete them.

* Trips you watch, so the server can follow them and send alerts. A watch ends on its own when the trip ends; finished watches are deleted after 30 days.

* Trips you plan or watch, with the time of day, so Transport can offer to make a repeated trip your commute. These are deleted after 90 days.

* A notification token (Android and web) if you turn on alerts, so the server can send them. The iPhone app uses notifications made on the phone and sends no token.

## What stays on your phone

Home and work places, starred stops, recent stops, your Ask conversation and settings stay on your phone and are removed when you delete the app.

## Location

Transport asks for your location only when you tap Stops near me, plan a trip from where you are, or ask Siri for the next train home. Your position is sent for that one request to find nearby stops or plan the trip, and it is not stored. Transport never uses location in the background.

## Questions you ask

Questions typed in Ask, or asked through Siri, are sent to the server to find the answer in live transport data. They are not stored and are not sent to any other company.

## Who else is involved

* Transport for NSW provides the live transport data. The server asks it for stops, departures and trips; no information about you is sent.

* Notifications on Android go through Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging; web notifications go through your browser's push service.

* Maps on Android load tiles from OpenFreeMap, and maps on iPhone come from Apple Maps. Like any website, these services see your phone's IP address when the map loads.

* The server runs on Cloudflare in the cloud.

## Children

Transport is a general audience app and does not knowingly collect information from children.

## Deleting your data

Delete saved trips and commutes in the app (swipe left on a trip), and stop watching a trip from the Watching tab. To have everything kept for your install removed from the server, email bidur@mail.tab.bot with the install ID shown at the bottom of Settings. It is removed within 30 days.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions: bidur@mail.tab.bot.
