# Privacy policy: Transport: Sydney Live

The text published at https://transportsydney.app/privacy/ (source: site/privacy/index.html). This copy is generated from that page; edit the page, then regenerate this file.

Updated 26 October 2026

This policy covers the Transport: Sydney Live app for iPhone and Android, this website and the server the apps use. The app and website are provided by Fintex Australia Pty Ltd (ABN 60 688 406 108), New South Wales, Australia ("we", "us"). Contact: privacy@transportsydney.app.

**The short version.** There are no accounts, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number. There are no ads, no analytics and no third party tracking, and nothing is sold or shared for advertising. This website sets no cookies and runs no scripts.

## What the server keeps
* **A random ID for your install.** The app makes it on your phone the first time it opens. It is not your device's advertising ID and is not linked to who you are. The server uses it to keep your saved trips, commutes, watched trips and alerts together.
* **Saved trips and commutes** you create, with their names and stops, until you delete them.
* **Trips you watch,** so the server can follow them and send alerts. A watch ends on its own when the trip ends; finished watches are deleted after 30 days.
* **Morning checks** you turn on: the two stations, the usual time and days, and the result of the last check, until you delete them.
* **Lift outage and car park alerts,** if you turn them on. Lift alerts keep the station numbers from your saved trips. A car park alert keeps which car park you asked about and ends after 3 hours.
* **Trips you plan or watch, with the time of day,** so the app can offer to make a repeated trip your commute. These are deleted after 90 days.
* **A last way home reminder,** if you set one: the trip home and its time (if you set it from where you are, that starting point instead of a stop), deleted 12 hours after that service.
* **A notification token** if you allow notifications, so the server can send alerts to your phone. It is deleted when you turn alerts off or delete all your data. On the iPhone the app also sends the phone's own notification token each time it opens; it is deleted when the app has not sent it for 30 days.
* **The trip card on the iPhone Lock Screen:** during a trip, the card's push token and the trip, so the server can keep the card up to date while your phone is locked. Both are deleted when the trip ends, and never kept longer than 24 hours.

## Storage on your device

The apps use your phone's storage only to make the app work: your install ID, home and work stations, starred and recent stops, saved trips, trip reminders, Ask history and settings. This stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Trip reminders are scheduled on your phone, and nothing about them is sent to the server. Speak alerts use your phone's own voice; nothing you hear is sent anywhere.

This website sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device and counts no visits.

## Location

The app asks for your location only when you use a feature that needs it, such as stops near you, planning a trip from where you are, showing where you are during a live trip, or working out when to leave. Your position is sent for that one request and is not stored. There are two exceptions, both your choice: a last way home reminder set from where you are keeps that starting point with the reminder (deleted 12 hours after that service), and a shared live trip can include your latest position (see Live trips and sharing). The app never uses location in the background.

## Calendar

Calendar leave by (iPhone build 208 and later) is off unless you turn it on in More. When it is on, the app reads your calendar on your phone, only while Home is open, to show when to leave for events in the next few hours that have a place. It only reads; it never adds, changes or deletes events. Your phone asks for permission first, and you can take it back in your phone's settings at any time.

Only two things are sent to our server: the place written in the event (for example "Barangaroo"), as a place search like the ones you type in Plan, and the trip query from your usual starting stop to that place, arriving by the start time. Neither carries your install ID, so neither is kept in your trip history or linked to your phone. The event's name, notes, people and calendar are never sent. Places found are kept on your phone for up to 30 days and are deleted when you turn the feature off or use Delete all my data.

Add to Calendar on a trip uses your phone's own new event sheet; nothing is added unless you tap Add.

## Live trips and sharing

During a live trip your position is used on your phone to put you on the map. It is not sent to the server unless you share the trip.

If you tap Share trip, the server keeps the trip, its progress (current leg, next stop, arrival time) and, only if you leave Include my location on, your latest position. Anyone with the link can see these until the trip ends. The shared trip is deleted when you end the trip or stop sharing, otherwise 30 minutes after the trip arrives, and never later than 6 hours after it starts.

## Questions you ask

Questions typed in Ask, or asked through Siri, are sent to the server to find the answer in live transport data. Ask works by fixed rules and does not use generative AI. The words are not stored and are not sent to any other company. When the answer is a trip, its from and to stops are kept like any trip you plan.

## Emails you send us

If you email us, we keep your message and your email address so we can answer. We delete messages within 12 months, sooner if you ask, and never add you to a mailing list.

## Who else is involved
* **Transport for NSW** provides the live transport data. The server asks it for stops, departures and trips; no information about you is sent.
* **Cloudflare** hosts this website and the server, and processes requests to deliver them and protect them from abuse. For us it keeps a short log of one request in ten for up to 7 days, so we can find faults: which part of the server was asked, the result and how long it took. The log does not keep what you searched for, your position or your install ID.
* **Expo, Apple and Google** deliver notifications from the server: Expo passes them on, Apple delivers them to iPhones and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to Android phones. Reminders you set are scheduled on the phone and do not go through them.
* **Maps:** the Android app loads map tiles from OpenFreeMap, and the iPhone app uses Apple Maps. Like any website, these services see your IP address when a map loads.

We use these services only to run the app.

## Children

The app is for a general audience and does not knowingly collect information from children.

## Your choices and deleting your data

You can delete your data yourself, at any time:
* **Everything:** in the app, open More, then Clear history, then Delete all my data. This deletes your saved trips, commutes, morning checks, watched trips, alerts, notification tokens, the Lock Screen trip card and trip history from the server, and clears the app's storage on your phone, straight away.
* **Trip history on the server:** More, then Clear history.
* **One trip or watch:** swipe left on it, or stop watching it in Watching.

You can also email privacy@transportsydney.app with the install ID shown in More and we will remove everything kept for your install within 30 days, or tell you what we hold.
