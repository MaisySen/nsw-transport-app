# Transport for NSW data attribution

Live times, stops, routes, trip plans, alerts and vehicle positions come from the Transport for NSW Open Data Hub
(https://opendata.transport.nsw.gov.au). The data is published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International licence (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

What the licence asks for, and where the app does it:

* Credit the source. Shown in the app (iPhone: Settings, footer under About; Android and web: Settings) and in both store descriptions.
* Do not suggest endorsement. The app and the store copy say it is independent and not made or endorsed by Transport for NSW. The app does not use the Transport for NSW name as its title or the Transport for NSW, Opal or Sydney Trains logos. Line colours and line names (T1, M1, 461) are part of the data.
* Note changes. Times are shown as delivered, marked Live (with its age), Timetable or Not tracking. "Usually N min late" is worked out by our server from the live feed over past weeks and always worded as usual, not as a live time.

Attribution text (use as written):

Short (in app and store copy):
Contains transport data from Transport for NSW, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0.

Long (support page and store description footer):
Contains transport information from Transport for NSW Open Data, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Transport is an independent app. It is not made, endorsed or supported by Transport for NSW. Times can change; check signs and announcements at your stop.

Maps:

* iPhone: Apple Maps (attribution shown by MapKit).
* Android: MapLibre with OpenFreeMap vector tiles built from OpenStreetMap data. The map shows the OpenStreetMap credit through its attribution button. Credit: Map data from OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), tiles by OpenFreeMap.

Open Data Hub terms of use also ask apps to keep within the API rate limits of their plan (the server counts every upstream call per day and stops background sampling well under the daily limit) and not to present the data as official.
