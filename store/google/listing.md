## Full description (4000 max)

Live Sydney and NSW public transport. Trains, metro, buses, light rail and ferries, with live times for when each service leaves.

Live departures you can trust
Every board refreshes itself and the minutes count down on their own. Each time says where it comes from: Live with how fresh it is, Timetable when a service sends no live data, or Not tracking when a bus stops reporting. A bus that never came is never marked as left.

Your next trip first
Add home and add work once. Home opens on your next trip as one big card: the countdown, line, platform or stand and the next two times. In the morning that is Home to Work, in the evening the reverse. Other trips sit below as slim rows, and one + adds a trip or a stop.

Buses
Search the stop number on the sign or a route number. See every stop on a route with its stop number and the live buses along it, filter a stop's board by route, see what recently departed, and get GET ON and GET OFF on your two stops.

Reminders on your days
Add several reminders to a saved trip, each at its own time on the weekdays you pick. They ring with the app closed.

See where it is
Tap a service to follow it on the map, with every stop, the next stop and how many stops away it is.

Watch a trip
Swipe on any service or tap Watch trip. Transport tells you when to leave, when it runs late, when the platform changes, and when a late leg means you will miss your connection, with the next way to get there.

Home screen widget
Put your next trip on the home screen with a live countdown, platform and any delay or trackwork notice. Pick any trip for each widget and tap to open it.

Live trip
Tap Start trip to follow your trip on a live map with the next stop, stops left and a countdown to your arrival. Get an alert one stop before yours and at each change. Share trip sends a link that others can open to follow along; it stops working when the trip ends.

Fares
Each trip shows a fare estimate for Adult, Child, Concession or Gold, per leg and in total, with peak and off peak prices.

Park and Ride and lifts
See live free spaces at Park and Ride car parks, lift outages and step free access at stations, and card top up machines near you.

Ask
Type a question like "next train Parramatta to Central", "is the T1 late" or "last train home" and get a short answer with live times you can tap.

No account
No sign up, no ads, no tracking. Saved trips belong to a random ID made on your phone.
