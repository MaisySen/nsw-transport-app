# Content rating questionnaire (IARC, Play Console)

Matches Android versionCode 5 with the over the air updates up to 24 Oct 2026. Answer again (a new questionnaire) if these answers change.

* Email for the rating certificate: the developer account email
* Category: All other app types (not a game, not social, not news)
* Violence: No
* Sexuality: No
* Language (profanity): No
* Controlled substances: No
* Gambling or simulated gambling: No
* Does the app let users interact or exchange content with other users? No (a Share trip link is view only: the other person sees the trip and, if the user chose, their position; nothing can be sent back)
* Does the app share the user's current physical location with other users? Yes. Share trip with "Include my location" turned on sends the user's live position to the people they send the link to, until the trip ends. It is off unless the user turns it on for that trip.
* Does the app allow users to purchase digital goods? No
* Is the app a web browser or search engine? No
* Does the app contain news content? No (service alerts come from Transport for NSW data, not news)
* Unrestricted internet access: No

Expected result: Everyone (ESRB), PEGI 3, General (ACB / Australia), USK 0, with the interactive element "Shares Location".

Target audience and content (Play, App content): ages 18 and over and 13 to 17. Not designed for children; not in the Families programme.
