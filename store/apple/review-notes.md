Notes (paste as written):

Transport shows live Sydney and New South Wales public transport times from Transport for NSW open data (CC BY 4.0). No login is needed and every screen works from any location.

How to test:
1. Home tab: tap Add home, search Strathfield and pick Strathfield Station. Tap Add work, search Town Hall and pick Town Hall Station. Home now shows the next services with live countdowns. Tap the trip to see earlier and later services.
2. Plan tab: type Central and open Central Station for a live departure board. Tap any departure to follow the vehicle on the map. Swipe left on a departure and tap Watch to get alerts for it (allow notifications when asked). Tap Plan a trip, pick Burwood and Strathfield, open a trip and tap Start trip for the live trip with a Lock Screen countdown. It ends when you arrive.
3. More tab, Ask: tap a suggested question, or type "next train Burwood to Central" or "is the T1 late".
4. Siri: after opening the app once, say "Next train home in Transport" or "Ask Transport". Home must be set (step 1) for the first phrase.
5. Widgets: long press the Home Screen, tap Edit, Add Widget, Transport. Lock Screen widgets and StandBy are also available.
6. Live Activity: on a stop board, touch and hold a departure and tap Follow on Lock Screen.

Location is optional and asked only when you tap Show stops near me. If the review device is outside NSW, nearby stops will be far away; search works anywhere.

Live times depend on the Transport for NSW feeds. Late at night (about 1am to 4am Sydney time) many lines have no services, so boards can be short.

Transport is an independent app. It is not made or endorsed by Transport for NSW.
