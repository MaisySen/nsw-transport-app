Privacy policy

Updated 24 October 2026. This policy covers the Transport app for iPhone and Android and the website and web app at nsw-transport-bot.sites.tab.bot.

Transport: Sydney Live is provided by Fintex Australia Pty Ltd (ABN 60 688 406 108), New South Wales, Australia. Contact: contact@fintex.au. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Fintex Australia Pty Ltd.

The short version. Transport has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or phone number. It has no ads and no third party tracking, and it sets no cookies. We count page views in a way that cannot identify you. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

What the server keeps

  • A random ID for your install. The app makes it on your phone or in your browser the first time it opens. It is not your device's advertising ID and is not linked to who you are. The server uses it to keep your saved trips, commutes, watched trips and alerts together.
  • Saved trips and commutes you create, with their names and stops, until you delete them.
  • Trips you watch, so the server can follow them and send alerts. A watch ends on its own when the trip ends; finished watches are deleted after 30 days.
  • Lift outage alerts and car park alerts, if you turn them on. For lift outage alerts the server keeps the station numbers from your saved trips, so it can tell you when a lift there is out. A car park alert keeps which car park you asked about and ends after 3 hours. Turn lift alerts off in Settings to delete the list.
  • Trips you plan or watch, with the time of day, so Transport can offer to make a repeated trip your commute. These are deleted after 90 days.
  • A notification token (Android and web) if you turn on alerts, so the server can send them. The iPhone app uses notifications made on the phone and sends no token.

Page view counts

The website and web app count page views with our own simple counter, not a third party analytics service. Each view adds one to a daily total for that page (for example "Trips" or "Download") and for the website that linked to us, if any. We do not store your IP address, browser details, install ID or any other identifier with these counts, and we use no cookies or device fingerprinting for them, so they cannot be linked to you or followed across visits. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, nothing is counted. The mobile apps do not count views.

Cookies and storage on your device

Transport sets no cookies. The web app uses your browser's local storage, and the mobile apps use the phone's storage, only to make the app work: your install ID, home and work places, starred and recent stops, saved trips, your Ask conversation and settings. This stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app or clear site data in your browser. Because nothing is used for tracking or advertising, there is no cookie banner.

Location

Transport asks for your location only when you tap Stops near me, turn on Show where I am during a live trip, plan a trip from where you are, choose stations by distance, or ask Siri for the next train home. Your position is sent for that one request to find nearby stops or plan the trip, and it is not stored. Transport never uses location in the background.

Live trips and sharing

When you tap Start trip and allow Show where I am, your position is used on your phone only, to put you on the map next to your train, bus, ferry or tram. It is not sent to the server and not stored. If you turn on get off alerts, the server follows the trip itself (the services and stops, not your position) to send them, as it does for a watched trip.

If you tap Share trip, the server keeps the trip, its progress (current leg, next stop, arrival time) and, only if you leave Include my location on, your latest position rounded to about a metre. Anyone with the link can see these until the trip ends. The shared trip, with every position in it, is deleted when you end the trip or stop sharing, and otherwise 30 minutes after the trip arrives, and never kept longer than 6 hours.

Questions you ask

Questions typed in Ask, or asked through Siri, are sent to the server to find the answer in live transport data. The words you type are not stored and are not sent to any other company. When the answer is a trip, its from and to stops are kept like any trip you plan.

Messages you send us

If you use the contact form or email us, we keep your message, the topic and the email address you give (if any) so we can answer and fix problems. We delete messages within 12 months, sooner if you ask. We never add you to a mailing list.

Who else is involved

  • Transport for NSW provides the live transport data. The server asks it for stops, departures and trips; no information about you is sent.
  • Notifications on Android go through Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging; web notifications go through your browser's push service.
  • Maps in the web app and on Android load tiles from OpenFreeMap (OpenStreetMap raster tiles when a browser cannot draw vector maps), and maps on iPhone come from Apple Maps. Like any website, these services see your IP address when the map loads.
  • The website and server run on Cloudflare, which processes requests to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

We only use these services to run Transport, and each of them protects the data it handles to the same or a higher standard than this policy.

Children

Transport is a general audience app and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can delete your data yourself, at any time, without asking us:

  • One saved trip: on the Trips screen, swipe left on it, or tap and hold, then tap Delete trip. Tap Edit at the top to change or delete several.
  • Recent trips and recent stops: tap Clear next to the heading, or swipe left on one item. These are kept only on your device.
  • Recent searches, stops and trips: Settings, Clear history.
  • Your trip history on the server: Settings, Clear history, Delete my trip history on server. This deletes the trips you searched for and the commute suggestions made from them, straight away.
  • Everything: Settings, Clear history, Delete all my data. This deletes your saved trips, commutes, watched trips, alerts, lift and car park alerts, push sign up and trip history from the server, and clears the app's storage on your device, straight away.

Stop watching a trip from the Watching tab. If you prefer, email contact@fintex.au with the install ID shown at the bottom of Settings and we will remove everything kept for your install within 30 days. You can also ask what we hold about your install, and we will tell you.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions: contact@fintex.au or the contact form.
