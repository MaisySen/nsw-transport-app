Transport

Live Sydney and NSW public transport for iPhone and Android: live departures, trips with leave now countdowns, alerts for the trips you watch, widgets and a question box called Ask. No account, no ads.

Open the web app

Getting started

  • On Trips, tap Add home and Add work. Mornings show Home to Work first, evenings the reverse.
  • Search any stop or station to see its live board. Tap a service to follow it on the map.
  • Swipe left on a service, or tap Watch trip, to get alerts when to leave, when it runs late and when the platform changes.
  • In Ask, type things like "next train Burwood to Central" or "is the T1 late".
  • iPhone: say "Next train home in Transport" or "Ask Transport" to Siri. You can make a shorter phrase in the Shortcuts app.

Widgets

iPhone: touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget and pick Transport. Touch and hold a widget and tap Edit Widget to choose a trip or stop. Lock Screen widgets and StandBy work the same way.

Android: touch and hold the home screen, tap Widgets, find Transport and drag Next trip onto the screen, then pick a trip.

Common questions

A time says Scheduled. That service is not sending live data right now, so the timetable time is shown. Live times are marked Live.

No alerts arrive. Allow notifications for Transport in your phone's settings, and keep the trip watched in the Watching tab.

Nearby stops are missing. Allow location for Transport while using the app. Transport only covers NSW.

Using a new phone. Saved trips belong to your install. Email us the install ID from Settings on the old phone and we can move them.

Deleting your data

Delete saved trips by swiping left on them, and stop watches in the Watching tab. To remove everything the server keeps for your install, email bidur@mail.tab.bot with the install ID from the bottom of Settings. See the privacy policy.
