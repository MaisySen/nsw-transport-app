Transport

Live Sydney trains, buses, metro, light rail and ferries. Watch a trip and it tells you when to leave.

Android

Early access
Download for Android
  1. Tap Download for Android.
  2. Open the file when it finishes, from the notification or your Downloads.
  3. Android asks you to allow installs from your browser the first time. Allow it, go back, then tap Install.
  4. Open Transport and tap Turn on alerts in Settings.

Transport is not on Google Play yet, so Android may tell you the app comes from outside Google Play. Only install it from this page. The app updates itself when it opens. When it is on Google Play, this page will link there.

On a computer? Scan this with your Android phone camera.

iPhone

Coming to the App Store
Open the web app
  1. Open this page in Safari and tap Open the web app.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Transport from your Home Screen and tap Turn on alerts in Settings.

The iPhone app with widgets, Live Activities and lock screen alerts is on its way to the App Store. This page will link there when it is listed.
