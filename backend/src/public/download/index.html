Transport

Live Sydney trains, buses, metro, light rail and ferries. Watch a trip and it tells you when to leave.

Android

App file
Download for Android
  1. Tap Download for Android. If Chrome asks, tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the file when it finishes, from the notification or your Downloads.
  3. If asked, allow Chrome to install apps, go back, then tap Install.
  4. Open Transport and tap Turn on alerts in Settings.

Play Protect may say it does not know this developer. Tap More details, then Install anyway. The app updates itself when it opens.

On a computer? Scan this with your Android phone camera.

iPhone

Preview in Expo Go
1. Get Expo Go, free 2. Open Transport in Expo Go
  1. Install Expo Go from the App Store and open it once.
  2. Come back here and tap Open Transport in Expo Go.
  3. Next time, open Expo Go and tap Transport under Recently opened.

Expo Go is a preview: alerts work while it is open, and it opens inside Expo Go rather than from its own icon. The full iPhone app with its own icon, lock screen alerts and widgets comes through TestFlight.

On a computer? Scan this with your iPhone camera, then tap the Expo Go banner.
