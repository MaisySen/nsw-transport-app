## Description
Transport shows Sydney and NSW public transport the way it is right now, not the way the timetable says.

Live departures
Every board updates itself every 15 seconds and the minutes count down on their own. Each time says Live or Scheduled, so you always know which is which.

See where it is
Tap any train, bus, metro, light rail or ferry to watch it move on the map, with its stops, the next stop, how many stops away it is and how full it is.

Trips that keep up
Plan A to B and get a live leave in countdown, every leg with its platform and delay, and how long you have to change.

It comes to you
Swipe left on any service, or tap Watch this trip, and get an alert when it is time to leave, when it runs late, when the platform changes, and before your stop. If a late train means you will miss your bus, it finds the next way and tells you.

Your commute, learned
Plan the same trip on two days and it offers to make it your commute, with a leave now alert every weekday and a heads up only when something is wrong.

Honest predictions
It records live times and shows how late a service usually runs at that time of day, always marked as a prediction.

Data from Transport for NSW open data. Not an official Transport for NSW app.
