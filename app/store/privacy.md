# Privacy policy

Transport does not ask for your name, email or phone number, and has no accounts, ads or third party tracking.

What the app stores
* A random device id made on your phone, so the server knows which watched trips and alerts belong to you.
* Your notification token, if you turn on alerts, so the server can send them.
* Trips you watch, trips you save, commutes, and the trips you search, so it can follow them, send alerts and suggest a commute.
* Starred stops and recent searches stay on your phone.

Location
If you tap Stops near me, your phone sends its position once to find the closest stops. It is not stored.

Who gets data
Live times come from Transport for NSW open data. Notifications go through Expo and Apple or Google push services. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

Deleting your data
Stop watching a trip and delete saved trips and commutes in Settings. Deleting the app removes the device id from your phone. Email bidur@mail.tab.bot to have everything for your device removed from the server.
