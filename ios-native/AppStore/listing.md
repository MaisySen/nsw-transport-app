# App Store listing

**Name:** Transport: Sydney Live
**Subtitle:** Live trains, buses and trips
**Bundle id:** bot.tab.nswtransport (widget: bot.tab.nswtransport.widget)
**Category:** Navigation (secondary: Travel)
**Age rating:** 4+

**Description**

Live public transport for Sydney and NSW, built to feel at home on iPhone.

See exactly when your train, bus, metro, light rail or ferry leaves, with live times that count down every second and a clear Live or Scheduled badge on every service. Tap any service to watch it move on the map, with its stops, the next stop and how full it is.

Plan a trip from A to B and get a live leave in countdown, each leg with its live delay, platform and change time. Watch a trip and the app tells you when to leave, when it runs late, when the platform changes and when your connection is at risk. Save your trips by name, star your stops, and put your next trip on the Home Screen and Lock Screen.

Data from Transport for NSW.

**Privacy (App Privacy answers)**

* Data used to track you: none.
* Data linked to you: none.
* Data not linked to you: a random device id the app makes, used only so the server can follow the trips you choose to watch. Location is used only when you tap Show stops near me, sent as coordinates for that one search.

**Privacy policy text**

Transport does not collect your name, email, phone number or contacts. The app creates a random id on your phone so the server can follow trips you ask it to watch; watched trips end on their own when the trip ends. If you tap Show stops near me, your location is sent once to find the closest stops. Live transport data comes from Transport for NSW.
