Getting started

  • On first launch, choose your home and work stations, or skip. Home then shows your next trip as the big card on top: home to work in the morning, work to home after midday. Hold any other trip and choose Show first to put it there instead.
  • Tap + at the top of Home to add a trip, star a stop, or set Home or Work.
  • Search any stop or station to see its live departures, and tap a service to follow it. For a bus stop you can type the stop number printed on the sign.
  • Plan a trip in Plan. Turn on Step free to avoid stations with a lift out of service.
  • Open a saved trip and scroll to Reminders to have your phone remind you on the days you choose.
  • Watch a trip to get alerts when to leave, when it runs late and when the platform changes.

Common questions

What do Live, Timetable and Not tracking mean?
Live is a time from the service's own live data, with how long ago it was updated. Timetable means the service is not sending live data, so the timetable time is shown in grey. Not tracking means live data stopped (for example a bus's position went quiet), so the timetable time is shown instead of a countdown.
No alerts or reminders arrive.
Allow notifications for the app in your phone's settings. The app asks the first time you set a reminder or watch a trip. For trip alerts, keep the trip in Watching.
Speak alerts are silent.
Speak alerts talk while the app is open during a trip. On the current iPhone beta, Silent mode mutes the speaker, so use headphones or turn Silent mode off. The next iPhone build speaks with Silent mode on.
Nearby stops are missing.
Allow location for the app while using it. The app covers NSW only.
I have a new phone.
Saved trips belong to your install. Email us the install ID from More on the old phone and we can move them. Reminders are kept on the phone, so set them again on the new one.

Deleting your data

In the app, open More, then Clear history, then Delete all my data. It works straight away and also clears the app's storage on your phone. You can also email support@transportsydney.app with your install ID. See the Privacy Policy.
