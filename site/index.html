What it does

  • Your next trip, first. Home opens on one big card: the countdown, line, platform or stand, whether it is running late, and the next two times. Your other trips sit below as slim rows, and one + adds a trip or a stop.
  • Times you can trust. Every countdown says where it comes from: Live with how fresh it is, Timetable when a service sends no live data, or Not tracking when a bus stops reporting. A bus that never showed up is never marked as left.
  • Buses done properly. Search the stop number on the sign (for example 2046100) or a route number. See every stop on a route with its stop number and the live buses along it, filter a stop's board by route, see what recently departed, and get GET ON and GET OFF marked on your two stops.
  • Plan any trip. Trains, metro, buses, ferries and light rail, from any station or stop, or from where you are. Step free trips avoid lifts that are out of service. Each trip shows an Opal fare estimate.
  • Follow your trip. Start trip shows a live map with the next stop and your arrival time, with alerts before your stop and at each change. On iPhone the trip also shows on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, and Speak alerts can read the alerts out loud.
  • Reminders on your days. Add several reminders to a saved trip, each at its own time on the weekdays you pick. They ring on your phone with the app closed, and the app asks to send notifications only when you set the first one.
  • Check my trip before I go. Set your usual leave time and days, and get one alert only when something is wrong: a delay, a cancellation, trackwork, a platform change or a lift out of service.
  • Ask in plain words. Type "next train Burwood to Central" or "is the T1 late" and get a short answer from live data. Ask follows fixed rules; it is not a chatbot.
  • On iPhone. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, Siri and Shortcuts ("Next train home in Transport"), Control Center buttons and an Apple Watch app.
