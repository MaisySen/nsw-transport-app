Transport information

Departure times, trip plans, alerts, occupancy, vehicle positions and other transport information come from Transport for NSW Open Data and may be delayed, incomplete or inaccurate. Services can change or be cancelled at short notice. The app shows whether a time is live or from the timetable, but we cannot promise any service will run as shown. Always check official signs, announcements and transportnsw.info before you travel, especially for the last service of the night.

Alerts and notifications

Leave now alerts, delay alerts, morning checks and other notifications are a convenience. They depend on live data, your phone, your network and push services outside our control, so they can arrive late or not at all. Do not rely on them alone where missing a service would cause serious problems.
